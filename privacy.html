Privacy Policy — Awakening Path

Last updated:

Data Collection

Awakening Path does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All training data, preferences, and profiles are stored exclusively on the user's device via localStorage.

Third-party Services

The app does not use cookies, analytics, advertising networks, or any third-party tracking services.

Permissions

If the user enables reminders, the app may request notification permission. This is used solely to deliver local reminders on the user's device and involves no data transmission.

Data Deletion

All data can be erased at any time from within the app (System Menu → Danger Zone → Reset All Data) or by clearing the browser/app storage.
